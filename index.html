隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-12

我们收集的信息 / Information we collect

无。本应用不收集、不传输任何个人信息。

None. This app does not collect or transmit any personal information.

应用做了什么 / What the app does

当您在 Windows 资源管理器预览窗格中单击一个文件（.md 及多种代码/文本文件）时，应用仅在本机读取该文件，并将其内容渲染为预览显示。整个过程不联网、不上传、不共享任何数据。

When you click a file (.md and various code/text files) in the Windows Explorer preview pane, the app reads that file locally and renders its content for preview only. The entire process is offline: no network access, no upload, no sharing of any data.

日志 / Logs

应用可能在本地"应用数据"目录写入运行日志，仅用于故障排查；日志不含个人信息，且不对外发送。

The app may write diagnostic logs to its local "AppData" directory for troubleshooting only. Logs contain no personal information and are never sent externally.

数据存储与保护 / Data storage and protection

因不收集个人信息，无云端存储。

Because no personal information is collected, there is no cloud storage.

信息共享 / Information sharing

我们不会向任何第三方披露个人信息（本应用无个人信息可披露）。

We do not disclose any personal information to third parties (the app holds no personal information to disclose).

您的控制 / Your control

您可随时卸载本应用；卸载后本地日志随应用数据一并清除。

You may uninstall the app at any time; upon uninstallation, local logs are removed together with the app data.

联系 / Contact

如有疑问，可通过应用商店留言联系开发者。

For any questions, contact the developer via the Microsoft Store listing.
